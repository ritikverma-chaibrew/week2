What is Touch Grass Tales?

A small, friendly reason to go outside. Whatever you photograph (a bench, a cat, a strange sign) becomes the cast of a short tale. Everything is written in simple English: short sentences and easy words, so young readers and English learners can enjoy it too.

👧 Kids and parents📚 Beginner English learners🏫 Teachers💻 Anyone who needs a screen break
How it works

Four steps, about 10 minutes

  1. Connect the AI once

    Paste a free Google AI Studio key (2 minutes), or use LM Studio on your computer. How?

  2. Go outside

    Get a fun mission, like "find something that looks like a face", and take 5 to 10 photos.

  3. Pick a mood and format

    Funny, spooky or mystery. Then a story, a poem or a comic.

  4. Read your tale

    Watch live progress while the AI writes. Read it like a book or as slides, and publish it to the gallery if you like.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Google AI Studio gives you a free key with Gemma models. You can also use LM Studio, which runs fully on your own computer.

Is my API key safe?

Your key stays in your browser for 5 hours and is then deleted automatically. It is sent with each request but the server never stores or logs it. You can press Forget at any time.

What happens to my photos?

Photos are shrunk, their hidden location data (EXIF) is removed, and they are deleted from the server after 24 hours. When you press Publish, your tale and small copies of its photos are saved to your browser's local storage, so you can find it again in the gallery on this device. It is never uploaded for others to see.

Why does it take a few minutes?

The AI works in small, careful steps: it looks at each photo, plans the plot, writes each part, then proofreads. Free keys have rate limits, so there is a short pause between steps.

Can I try it without a key?

Yes. The demo shows a pre-made walk with no setup, and the gallery has sample tales.
